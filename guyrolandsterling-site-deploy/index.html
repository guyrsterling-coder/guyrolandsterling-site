Reflections
A Path Less Traveled

"For All The Lonely Hearts" didn't start with a broken heart. It started with a cave.

In 1 Kings 19, Elijah has just seen fire fall from heaven — the biggest win of his life. Days later, he's alone in the wilderness, running for his life, asking God to let him die. He finds a cave and hides in it.

A windstorm tears through. God's not in it. An earthquake. Not there either. A fire. Still nothing.

Then — a whisper. And that's where Elijah finally hears him.

The mountain to the cave. Certainty to alone — in what couldn't have been more than a few days. Nothing outward even changed. That's not just Elijah's story. That's most of us, most weeks.

This song came from sitting with that gap. Not the fire. Not the mountain. The cave. The whisper — the part that never makes the highlight reel, but somehow, it's where he shows up.

I've sat in a cave or two of my own. Some of what I found there lives in my reflections.
